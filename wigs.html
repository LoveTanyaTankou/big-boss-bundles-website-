Questions and Answers

Luxury Wig FAQ

Do all Big Boss Bundles wigs include HD lace?

Yes. Every wig in this collection includes HD lace. Customers do not need to choose a separate lace type.

What density options are available?

Available density options are 180%, 200%, 220%, 250%, and 300%. The featured density is 250%.

What is the difference between Glueless and Glue Required?

A Glueless wig is designed for secure wear without adhesive when properly fitted. A Glue Required construction is intended for customers who prefer an adhesive installation.

Can the wigs be colored?

Human hair wigs may be customized by a qualified professional. Results depend on the selected starting color, hair condition, and coloring process.

How should I care for my wig?

Use products made for premium human hair, detangle gently, minimize excessive heat, and store the wig properly between wears.

How long does fulfillment take?

Unless the wig is available for local pickup, standard fulfillment is generally 7 to 10 business days.
