Important Local Pickup Information

Placing an order online does not guarantee that your item is currently available at our Pflugerville location for immediate pickup.

You must call ahead to confirm that your selected item, length, texture, color, or other options are available in store and that your order is ready for pickup.

Online orders selected for local pickup may require 7–10 days before they are ready, even for local customers. Custom orders, custom colors, and certain product selections may require additional preparation time.

Please do not come to the store to pick up an online order until you have received confirmation that your order is ready.
