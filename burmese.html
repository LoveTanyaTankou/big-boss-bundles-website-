Protect Your Investment

Burmese Hair Care

Wash and Condition

Use moisturizing, sulfate-free products. Detangle gently from the ends upward and avoid aggressive rubbing or twisting.

Protect at Night

Wrap or braid the hair loosely and use a satin bonnet or pillowcase to reduce friction, tangling and dryness.

Use Heat Carefully

Apply a heat protectant before styling and keep temperatures moderate to help protect the natural integrity of the hair.

Maintain the Texture

Use lightweight products and avoid heavy buildup. Curly and wavy textures benefit from gentle hydration and finger styling.
