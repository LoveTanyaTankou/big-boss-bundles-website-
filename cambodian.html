Before You Order

Cambodian Hair FAQ

Which lengths are available?

Bundles are offered from 12 to 32 inches. Select wig styles may be offered up to 40 inches, depending on availability.

What lace is used?

Big Boss Bundles wigs, closures and frontals use HD lace for a natural-looking finish.

How long does fulfillment take?

Unless an item is confirmed for local pickup, allow approximately 7 to 10 business days for fulfillment.

What is the return policy?

All sales are final. Eligible exchanges must be requested within five days after receiving the order and the hair must remain unused and unaltered.
