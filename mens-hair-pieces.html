How to Measure Your Hair System

Measure the area that needs coverage rather than your entire head. Measure from your desired front hairline to the back of the coverage area, then measure side to side across the widest part.

Customize Your Hair System

Cut & Blend

Human hair systems can be professionally cut, shaped and blended with your existing hair. Clippers and professional shears can be used to create fades, tapers and other finished styles after installation.

Color & Dye

Human hair can be professionally colored to help achieve the desired shade and blend. Professional coloring is recommended because aggressive bleaching or repeated chemical processing may shorten the life of the hair system.

Application

Hair systems may be installed using professional hair-system adhesive or hair-system tape. The scalp should be properly cleaned and prepared before installation. Carefully position the system at the desired hairline and secure it according to the bonding product's instructions.
