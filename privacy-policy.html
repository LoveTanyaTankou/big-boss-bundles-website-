Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Big Boss Bundles collects, uses, protects, and shares information when you visit our website, place an order, register for a class, or contact us.

Last Updated: September 19, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Big Boss Bundles website, make a purchase, register for a Beauty Academy class, join a waiting list, request customer support, or otherwise interact with us, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide.

This information may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Billing and shipping address
  • Order and purchase information
  • Product selections and preferences
  • Class registration information
  • Messages or information you provide when contacting us

2. Payment Information

Payments made through our website may be processed by third party payment service providers. Big Boss Bundles does not need to directly store your complete payment card information when payment information is securely processed by the applicable payment provider.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to operate our business and provide our products and services.

This may include using information to:

  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Arrange shipping or local pickup
  • Process Beauty Academy registrations
  • Manage class availability and waiting lists
  • Communicate about purchases or registrations
  • Provide customer service
  • Respond to questions and requests
  • Maintain and improve our website and services
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, or unauthorized transactions
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

4. Cookies and Website Information

Our website and service providers may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, remember certain preferences, understand website usage, maintain shopping cart functions, and improve the customer experience.

Your browser may allow you to control or disable certain cookies. Disabling cookies may affect some website features.

5. How Information May Be Shared

We may share information with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate our business and provide requested services. These may include payment processors, website and hosting providers, shipping providers, and other companies that help us fulfill orders or operate our website.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, customers, business, or property.

Big Boss Bundles does not sell customer personal information for money.

6. Order and Transaction Records

We may maintain records relating to orders, payments, shipping, local pickup, returns, customer service communications, class registrations, and other transactions for legitimate business, accounting, fraud prevention, and legal purposes.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information. However, no website, electronic transmission, or data storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

9. Children's Privacy

Our online store is not intended to collect personal information directly from young children without appropriate involvement from a parent or legal guardian.

Some Beauty Academy programs may be available to minors. Information associated with a minor's registration should be submitted by or with the involvement of the minor's parent or legal guardian when required.

10. Third Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to third party websites or use services provided by third parties. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Big Boss Bundles is not responsible for the privacy practices of third party websites that we do not control.

11. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us regarding questions about personal information you have provided to Big Boss Bundles or to request that we review or correct information where appropriate.

Certain information may need to be retained for completed transactions, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy may be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Big Boss Bundles handles personal information, please contact us.
